## 2. Hospital Profile

MediCore Hospital is a 420-bed adult tertiary teaching hospital in Harbor Vale (fictional), founded in 1987. Principal units: 60-bay Emergency Department (DEP-001), 36-bed Intensive Care Unit (DEP-009), 12-bed stroke unit within Neurology (DEP-008), cardiac catheterization suite (DEP-003), heart-failure clinic (DEP-003), anticoagulation clinic (DEP-010). Information systems: MediChart (EHR), LabLink (laboratory), PharmaSafe (pharmacy and interaction alerts).
